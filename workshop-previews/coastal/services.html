Start with what needs attention. We’ll show you what information helps with each type of upholstery enquiry.

Marine upholstery

Interior and exterior boat upholstery for cabin seating, deck cushions and other upholstered pieces on board.

For an estimate: Include the boat make or model if you know it, and a wide photo showing where the piece fits.

Start a marine enquiry

Antique auto & motorcycle

Seats for antique cars and motorcycles. A photo of the existing seat helps show its shape, seams and what you’d like to keep.

For an estimate: Include year and model if known and a close-up of the worn section.

Start a project enquiry

Leather repair

Leather repairs and patch sewing. A small tear is a different enquiry from an entire seat that needs recovering.

For an estimate: Include one wide photo and one close-up of the damage.

Start a project enquiry

Commercial seating

Banquette seating and upholstery for medical equipment such as dental chairs and chiropractic tables.

For an estimate: Include the number of pieces and tell us if they can come out separately.

Start a project enquiry
